A skill on your record is permanent. One given by an admin for a single operation lasts through respawns and ends with the mission.

## The skills

| Tag | Skill | What it does | Custom arsenal |
|---|---|---|---|
| MED | Medic | ACE medic, full | Medic bags, surgical kit, PAK, medical drone |
| BRC | Breacher / EOD | ACE explosives specialist | Charges, mines, defusal kit, detectors |
| SNP | Sniper | - | AI AXMC rifles and long optics |
| MKS | Marksman | - | HK417 rifles and long optics |
| JFO | JFO | Joint fires: calls CAS, artillery and mortars (9-line) | - |
| ENG | Engineer | ACE engineer | - |
| ISR | ISR | ISR flag | ISR list |
| UAV | UAV operator | Drone hacking | - |
| CLS | Combat Lifesaver | ACE medic, basic | - |

## Your arsenal

Each layer adds to the one before:

1. **Common** - everyone: rifles, ammunition, uniforms and kit
2. **Platoon** and **squad** - if your platoon or squad has its own list
3. **Role** - the slot's own extras
4. **Skills** - a custom arsenal for each skill you hold

Granted a skill in the field? Its gear is there the next time you open the arsenal.

## Everyone sees your skills

Your skills show as tags, most telling first. On the tacpad squad list a medic reads **MED**; on the HUD, the map and the slot menu your name carries them: *Reyes (MED)*.
