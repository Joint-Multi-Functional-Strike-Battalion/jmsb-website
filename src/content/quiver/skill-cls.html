What the skill is

Stops the bleeding and keeps an airway open until the medic arrives; carries the Medic Bag.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as CLS on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Tourniquets, pressure bandages, trauma dressings and chest seals, done fast and under fire.
  • OPA and NPA airways, the suction bag, needle decompression, the AED.
  • The elastic wrap, splints and a look for fractures; ammonia inhalant and the autoinjectors.

What it unlocks

  • The Medic Bag (the CLS load) on top of the Boo Boo Bag everyone carries.

What you need to know

  • MARCH, and where the lifesaver stops and the medic starts.
  • Soldier first aid, then Combat Lifesaver: MARCH, MIST, CASEVAC and the lifesaver's part of Drugs and CBRN.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
