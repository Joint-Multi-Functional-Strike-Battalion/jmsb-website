What the skill is

Works away from the team: reconnaissance, precision fire at range, and the patience both take.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as SNP on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Operates as a pair with a spotter; reports as much as shoots.

What it unlocks

  • The sniper arsenal: the sniper system, the spotting scope, the ghillie.

What you need to know

  • Sniper: mission, qualifications, ballistics, equipment.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
