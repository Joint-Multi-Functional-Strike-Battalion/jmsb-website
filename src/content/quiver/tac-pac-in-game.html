The PAC tile. Four tabs:

  • MY RECORD - rank, role, skills, awards, status, time on.
  • ROSTER - tap a name to read that man's record.
  • OPORD - the current order, paragraph by paragraph, with the ROE.
  • REQUESTS - raise one (leave, an award recommendation, a problem); an admin sees it on the web.

    Not on the roster yet? The tile offers APPLY TO JOIN. Everything else about your record is changed on pac.jmsb.info.
