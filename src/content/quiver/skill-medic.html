What the skill is

Everything that goes inside: advanced airways, chest tubes, lines, drugs and fractures.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as MED on the squad panel.

What you do

  • The i-gel, the cric kit and suction; chest tubes and thoracostomy.
  • IV and IO lines, blood, plasma and saline; every drawn-up drug.
  • Fracture realignment; ACM's evacuation.

What it unlocks

  • The Trauma, Fluid and Drug kits and the medic backpacks; the medical UAV.

What you need to know

  • Medic: MARCH end to end, then Drugs, Evacuation, CBRN and Items by level.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
