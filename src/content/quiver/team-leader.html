What the seat is

Command, comms, higher coordination, reconnaissance handoff. Always slot 1 - the element leader.

Every team - Ghost, Nomad, Raider and the Support Team - has one. Make the rank and you can take it: the slot asks for Sergeant.

What you do

  • Take the tasking from Overlord and turn it into a plan the team can run.
  • Run the team net; keep the company net informed with the ready-made reports (SITREP, CONTACTREP, CASEVAC) from the tablet.
  • Call for what the team cannot do itself: fires, air, transport, the drones.
  • Decide when to push, when to hold and when to pull back. The team moves on your word.

What you carry

  • The team-lead vest and a laser designator; two radios (PRC-148 and PRC-152) and a PRC-117F in the pack for the company net.
  • Radio nets: C2, C2.reports, FIRES.cas, FIRES.arty, FIRES.trans, MEDICAL.medevac, ENGINEER, GHOST, NOMAD, RAIDER, SUPPORT.
  • Tablet tiles: drones, jam, support, hack, weather, timer, radio.

What you need to know

  • The standing orders, cold.
  • The radio procedure and every report format.
  • Formations, movement techniques and reaction to contact (Combat skills).
  • How to call fires and air (Fires).

How you get here

Make Sergeant. There is no course: lead a fire team under a team leader first, then take the seat when it is open.
