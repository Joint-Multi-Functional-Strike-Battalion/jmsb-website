What the skill is

Opens what is shut and makes safe what is left behind: charges, mines, IEDs.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as BRC on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Breaching charges on doors, walls and wire.
  • Finding, marking and disarming mines and IEDs; placing our own.

What it unlocks

  • The breacher arsenal: charges, detonators, the mine detector, the defusal kit.

What you need to know

  • Engineering: explosive charges, mines, IEDs, detonators.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
