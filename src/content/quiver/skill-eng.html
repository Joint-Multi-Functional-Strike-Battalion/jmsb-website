What the skill is

Keeps the vehicles running: repair, refuel, rearm, and the explosives that are not a breacher's.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as ENG on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Field repair of wheels, tracks and hull; refuel and rearm from the logistics trucks.
  • Vehicle recovery and the repair facilities.

What it unlocks

  • The engineering tools; what else is on the Engineering page.

What you need to know

  • Engineering: vehicles, repair, refuel, rearm.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
