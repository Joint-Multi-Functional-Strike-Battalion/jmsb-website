What the skill is

Flies the drones: the team's reach over the next hill and the strike that goes with it.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as UAV on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Launches, flies and recovers the unit's drones from the tablet; hands the picture to the team leader and the JFO.
  • Keeps the drone alive: battery, altitude, the jammers.

What it unlocks

  • The UAV terminal and the drone backpacks.

What you need to know

  • The drones tile on the tablet; the jamming page.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
