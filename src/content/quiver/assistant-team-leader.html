What the seat is

Second in command - runs the net if the TL drops, senior scout, manages the trail element.

The slot asks for Corporal.

What you do

  • Take the team the moment the team leader goes down or off the net - you are on every net he is.
  • Run the trail element on the move and the rear on the halt; count heads, count ammunition, keep the casualties moving.
  • Be the senior scout: the first set of eyes forward when the team leader is planning.

What you carry

  • The rifleman vest, binoculars and a Vector in the pack; the same two radios as the team leader.
  • Radio nets: C2, C2.reports, FIRES.cas, FIRES.arty, FIRES.trans, MEDICAL.medevac, GHOST, NOMAD, RAIDER, SUPPORT.
  • Tablet tiles: drones, jam, hack, weather, timer, radio, support, pac.

What you need to know

  • Everything the team leader knows - you will be him before the night is out.
  • The MIST and CASEVAC reports by heart; the assistant runs the casualty.

How you get here

Make Corporal and show you can keep a fire team together under contact.
