What the skill is

The team's long gun inside the team: precise fire out to where the rifles stop.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as MKS on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Precision fire in support of the team's movement; the first look at what is out there.

What it unlocks

  • The marksman arsenal: the designated marksman rifle and its optics.

What you need to know

  • Heavy weapons: aiming, mils and MOA, range estimation.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
