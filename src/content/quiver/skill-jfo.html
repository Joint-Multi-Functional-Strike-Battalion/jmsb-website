What the skill is

Puts the company's fires where the team leader wants them: artillery, mortars, close air.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as JFO on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Calls for fire from the tablet's support page and adjusts them onto the target.
  • Talks the aircraft on; keeps the team clear of its own fires.

What it unlocks

  • The laser designator and the Vector; the fires nets.

What you need to know

  • Indirect fire and the Vector 21.

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
