The motorpool

ACE interact on the motorpool object → Motorpool. Tabs: CARS, ARMOR, MECHANIZED, HELI, PLANE, BOAT, STATIC, SPAWNED. Pick one, read its armament, countermeasures and top speed, CUSTOMIZE the texture and fittings, then SPAWN VEHICLE. It arrives fuelled and armed. REMOVE VEHICLE takes an empty one away.

In the staging zone

Sitting in the vehicle inside a staging zone, ACE self-interact → Vehicle Staging Zone:

  • Repair and Refuel
  • Regear - ammunition back to full
  • Vehicle Cosmetics - the skins
  • Vehicle Loadouts - pylon presets
