What the skill is

Intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance: the eyes the plan is built on.

A skill is yours wherever you sit: it is on your TAC//PAC record and comes with you into any slot. In game it shows as ISR on the squad panel.

What you do

  • Runs the reconnaissance before and during the operation; reports what the enemy is, where and doing what.
  • Works the sensors and the recon drones with the UAV operator.

What it unlocks

  • The ISR arsenal: optics, sensors, the recon kit.

What you need to know

  • Land navigation and the report formats (SPOTREP, CONTACTREP).

How you get it

By doing it. There is no course to pass first: show you can do the job on an operation and staff put the skill on your record. If you want to be taught, ask - we will make the time.
